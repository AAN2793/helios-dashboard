## 💡 Recommendations

### 1. Add More Engagement Triggers
- **Ask questions**: "What are you watching?" or "Is this the top?"
- **Polls**: "Will $SPY hold 500? Yes/No"
- **Call to action**: "Join the room - link in bio"

### 2. Proof Posts (High Conversion)
- Share specific trade results with % gains
- "Member alert on $INO hit +40%"
- Tag members (with permission)

### 3. Timely News + Your Take
- Not just headline - add YOUR analysis
- "Here's why this matters for [sector]"
- Position yourself as the expert

### 4. Controversy/Take
- Contrarian views perform well
- "Why [mainstream take] is wrong"
- "Market is ignoring [x]"

### 5. Visual Content
- Charts/screenshots get more engagement
- Consider posting with images of setups

## 2. South Dakota Corporate Veil Protection

### General Rule
South Dakota has **strong LLC asset protection statutes**. Courts generally respect LLC liability shields.

### Piercing the Veil - When It Happens
SD courts can pierce corporate veil for:
1. **Fraud**: Intentional misrepresentation to creditors
2. **Alter Ego**: No separation between company/personal affairs
3. **Undercapitalization**: Grossly insufficient capital for operations
4. **Failure to observe formalities**: No meetings, no records

### Debt-Specific Concerns
**Has SD pierced due to debt?**
- No published cases specifically for debt liability
- Piercing cases typically involve fraud, not ordinary debt
- Properly capitalized LLC with separate accounts = strong protection

### Recommendations
- Maintain separate business accounts (never commingle)
- Adequate capitalization ($60k is reasonable for startup)
- Annual member meetings with minutes
- Proper insurance (see Section 3)
- Signed operating agreement (with buy-sell provisions)

## 4. LLC Asset Protection for Capital Raising

### Current Structure Weaknesses
Single SD LLC exposes all assets to any liability

### Recommended Structure Options

**Option A: Series LLC (Simplest)**
- SD allows Series LLCs
- Each well/plug job = separate series
- Liability isolated per series
- Lower admin burden

**Option B: Holding Company Structure**
```
Carbon Cut Holdings (SD Parent LLC)
├── Carbon Cut Operations (Operating LLC)
├── Carbon Cut Equipment (Asset LLC)  
└── Carbon Cut Real Estate (Property LLC)
```
- Asset protection between divisions
- Cleaner for investor due diligence
- Flexibility to sell divisions separately

**Option C: Special Purpose Entities**
- Each major contract = new LLC
- Maximum liability isolation
- Higher admin/legal costs

### Recommendation
**Start with Series LLC**, migrate to holding company structure as you scale beyond $500k annual revenue.

### Capital Raise Considerations
1. **Investor protection**: Clean cap table, documented valuations
2. **Regulatory compliance**: Securities filings if raising >$1M
3. **Operating agreement updates**: Preemptive rights, dilution protections
4. **Escrow structure**: Milestone-based releases for well plugging contracts
